📸 Product Image Guidelines

Quick Summary

Our system automatically optimizes all product images. Follow these guidelines for best results:

👤 Portrait Images (People)

Recommended Size:

600 × 800 pixels

3:4 aspect ratio (width:height)

When to use:

  • Fitness/gym apparel worn by models
  • Beauty products (faces, close-ups)
  • Any product showing human subjects

✓ Shows full body/face without cropping

🏋️ Landscape Images (Equipment)

Recommended Size:

800 × 600 pixels

4:3 aspect ratio (width:height)

When to use:

  • Gym equipment and machines
  • Flat lay product photos
  • Wide product arrangements

✓ Ideal for equipment and wider products

📦 Square Images (Versatile)

Recommended Size:

600 × 600 pixels

1:1 aspect ratio

When to use:

  • When you're unsure about orientation
  • Products that look good either way
  • Logo or centered product shots

✓ Safe fallback for any product type

⚙️ Technical Requirements
File Specifications
  • Maximum Size: 10 MB
  • Formats Supported:
    • JPG / JPEG
    • PNG
    • WebP
    • HEIC (Apple)
Resolution Guidelines
  • Minimum Width: 400 pixels
  • Recommended Width: 600-800 pixels
  • Images larger than 1200px width will be automatically resized.
✅ What We Automatically Do For You
  • EXIF Rotation Correction

    Mobile phone orientation automatically fixed. Portrait stays portrait, landscape stays landscape.

  • Format Conversion

    All images converted to optimized WebP format. File size reduced by ~80% while maintaining quality.

  • Image Optimization

    Automatically resized to optimal display size (max 1200px width). Quality: 80/100.

  • Thumbnail Generation

    Compact 300px thumbnails auto-created for product galleries and checkout.

✓ DO THIS
  • ✓ Use recommended dimensions (600×800, 800×600, or 600×600)
  • ✓ Center important content (face, logo, product)
  • ✓ Use high-quality photos (not compressed)
  • ✓ Show clear product details and features
  • ✓ Upload from mobile? No problem! Rotation is automatic
  • ✓ Use consistent lighting for product photos
✗ AVOID THIS
  • ✗ Very small images (<400px width)
  • ✗ Important content near top/bottom edges
  • ✗ Files larger than 10 MB
  • ✗ Blurry or low-contrast photos
  • ✗ Extremely wide aspect ratios (>2:1)
  • ✗ Images with text (hard to read when optimized)

💡 Best Practices for Product Photos
Lighting & Setup
  • Use natural daylight when possible
  • Avoid harsh shadows from direct sunlight
  • Place product against a clean background
  • Use a tripod for stable, level photos
Composition
  • Fill 70% of frame with the product
  • Leave small margins around important content
  • Use rule of thirds for balanced composition
  • Show product from flattering angles
Editing (Optional)
  • Adjust brightness/contrast if needed
  • Correct color balance to match real product
  • Avoid over-filtering or heavy effects
  • Remove dust/imperfections with clone tool
Mobile Photography
  • ✓ Hold phone steady (portrait orientation)
  • ✓ Clean camera lens before shooting
  • ✓ Use grid lines for composition help
  • ✓ Our system auto-corrects your phone's rotation
