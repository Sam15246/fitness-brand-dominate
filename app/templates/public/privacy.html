Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information.

Last Updated: February 2026
We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to process your orders and improve our service:

1.1 Directly Provided Information

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address
  • Address Information: Street address, city, postal code, state
  • Optional Information: Your opt-in preferences for communications

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, browser, operating system
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, products viewed, time spent
  • Technical Data: IP address, referrer URL, timestamps

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Courier Partners: Delivery status, location data (via WhatsApp)
  • WhatsApp: Message timestamps and communication records

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

2.1 Order Fulfillment

  • Processing and confirming your orders
  • Shipping products to your address
  • Calculating shipping costs and taxes
  • Generating invoices and order receipts

2.2 Customer Communication

  • Sending order confirmations and tracking updates via WhatsApp
  • Responding to customer inquiries
  • Providing customer support
  • Notifying about order status changes

2.3 Service Improvement

  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve our website
  • Personalizing your shopping experience
  • Identifying and preventing fraud
  • Complying with legal obligations

2.4 Marketing (with your consent)

  • Sending promotional messages about new products
  • Sharing special offers and discounts
  • Requesting feedback or reviews

3. Your Consent

By placing an order on our website, you consent to:

  • Collection and processing of your personal data as described
  • Use of your phone number for WhatsApp communication
  • Sharing your address with courier partners for delivery
  • Storage of your data in our database

4. Data Sharing

Your personal data may be shared with:

4.1 Trusted Third Parties

  • Courier Partners: Shipping address, name, phone for delivery
  • WhatsApp: Your phone number and message content
  • Payment Processors: (if applicable) Only transaction details needed

4.2 NOT Shared With

  • Third-party marketers or advertisers
  • Analytics companies (beyond internal analytics)
  • Any party outside India without your consent
  • Any party for commercial gain without permission

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all web traffic
  • Secure database with restricted access
  • Regular security updates
  • Regular backups to prevent data loss
  • Staff training on data privacy

Note: While we implement security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for the following periods:

  • Order & Shipping Data: 7 years (for tax/legal compliance)
  • Customer Communications: 3 years (for dispute resolution)
  • Account Data: Until account deletion or inactivity (5+ years)
  • Website Usage Data: 1 year (for analytics)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-Out: Stop receiving promotional messages anytime
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp with your request.

8. Opt-Out of Communications

If you don't want to receive promotional messages:

  1. Send a message via WhatsApp: "STOP PROMOTIONS"
  2. Uncheck opt-in during checkout
  3. Click "unsubscribe" on any promotional email

Note: We'll continue sending transactional messages (order updates, shipping notifications) even if you opt out of promotions.

9. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses:

  • Session Cookies: To keep you logged in
  • Persistent Cookies: To remember your preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: (Optional) To understand usage patterns

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, some website features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., WhatsApp, social media):

  • We are not responsible for their privacy practices
  • Review their privacy policies before sharing information
  • We have no control over external sites

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 years old:

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
  • If we discover we have, we'll delete it immediately
  • Parents should monitor their children's internet activity

12. International Data

All data is stored and processed within India:

  • We currently serve only Indian customers
  • Data is not transferred internationally
  • Your data is protected by Indian laws

13. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time:

  • Changes will be posted on this page
  • Significant changes will be notified via email
  • Your continued use means you accept changes
  • We recommend reviewing this policy periodically

14. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach:

  • We'll notify affected users via email/WhatsApp immediately
  • We'll inform authorities as required by law
  • We'll provide information on what data was compromised
  • We'll suggest steps you can take to protect yourself

15. Legal Requests

We may disclose your information if required by:

  • Law enforcement (with warrant or court order)
  • Government agencies
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Protection of our rights or safety

16. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data:

  • Contact us via WhatsApp
  • Include "Privacy Inquiry" in your message
  • We'll respond within 48 hours

Your Trust Matters: We take data privacy seriously. We'll never sell your information or use it for purposes you didn't agree to.
