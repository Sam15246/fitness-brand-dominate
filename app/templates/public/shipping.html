Shipping Policy

Fast, reliable delivery across India.

Shipping Regions

We currently ship across all of India (All states and union territories). International shipping is not available at this time.

Delivery Timeline

  • Processing Time: 1-2 business days after order confirmation
  • Typical Delivery: 3-7 business days from shipment
  • Remote Areas: 7-14 business days (higher altitude or tribal regions)

Note: Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary based on weather, local holidays, and logistical factors beyond our control.

Shipping Partners

We work with reliable logistics partners including:

  • Delhivery
  • Blue Dart
  • DTDC
  • Regional logistics networks

Shipping Cost

FREE Shipping on all orders across India (no minimum order value).

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive:

  • Tracking number via WhatsApp
  • Courier partner name
  • Real-time tracking link

You can also log in to your account and check order status anytime.

Packaging

All items are securely packed to prevent damage during transit. We use:

  • Protective bubble wrap
  • Sturdy cardboard boxes
  • Sealed packaging to prevent tampering

Damaged Items

If your product arrives damaged:

  1. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp
  2. Provide photos of the damaged item and packaging
  3. We'll arrange a replacement or refund

Lost Packages

In rare cases where a package is lost during transit:

  • We will file a claim with the courier
  • Replacement will be sent once claim is settled
  • If claim is denied, we'll discuss options with you directly

Refused or Undelivered Orders

If you refuse delivery or the package cannot be delivered:

  • The package will be returned to us
  • Refund will be processed after return is received and inspected
  • Return shipping cost may apply in some cases

Delivery Issues?

If your package has not arrived within 7 days of shipment:

  1. Check your tracking status first
  2. Contact us via WhatsApp with your order number
  3. We'll investigate with the courier and follow up
  4. Resolution within 48 hours

💡 Tip: Always save your tracking number. You'll need it if a package goes missing.

Questions?

Contact us on WhatsApp for any shipping-related queries.
