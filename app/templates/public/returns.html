Support

Returns & Refunds

We stand behind the quality of every product.

Return Window

You have 30 days from the delivery date to return any product if you're not satisfied with it.

Important: Return requests must be initiated within 30 days. After 30 days, returns cannot be processed.

Product Condition Requirements

To be eligible for return, the product must:

  • Be unused and in original condition
  • Have all original packaging and materials
  • Have no visible signs of wear or damage (caused by user)
  • Not be damaged due to misuse or improper handling
  • Include all accessories that came with the product

Items NOT Eligible for Return

The following cannot be returned:

  • Custom or made-to-order items (unless defective)
  • Items with obvious signs of use or wear
  • Damaged items caused by misuse, dropping, or improper storage
  • Items returned after 30 days
  • Items without original packaging materials

Defective Products

If your product arrives defective or damaged during shipping, you can initiate a return at any time (not limited to 30 days) by providing:

  • Photos showing the defect clearly
  • Description of the issue
  • Your order number

For defective products, we'll arrange a replacement at no additional cost or issue a full refund.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp: Send your order number and reason for return at WhatsApp
  2. Get Return Authorization: We'll confirm your return request and provide a Return Authorization Number (RAN)
  3. Ship the Item: Pack the product securely in the original packaging with the RAN clearly written outside
  4. Provide Tracking: Send us the shipment tracking number via WhatsApp
  5. Inspection: Once received, we'll inspect the item and confirm receipt within 2-3 days

Return Shipping

Return shipping costs the responsibility of the customer (unless the item is defective or damaged during our shipping):

  • For defective items: We'll provide a pre-paid return label
  • For change of mind: Customer arranges and pays for return shipping

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your returned item:

  1. Item will be inspected for condition
  2. Refund will be approved (usually within 2 days)
  3. Amount will be credited back to your original payment method
  4. Processing time: 5-7 business days to reflect in your account

Note: Refunds cannot be issued to a different account than the original purchase.

Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be issued (10-20% reduction) if:

  • The item shows minor wear despite claims of being unused
  • Original packaging is opened but item is in good condition
  • Minimal signs of testing/handling are visible

Exchanges

If you need a different size, color, or model, we can arrange an exchange:

  • Submit exchange request within 30 days of delivery
  • Return original item in unused condition
  • If new item costs more: Pay the difference
  • If new item costs less: Store credit for the difference
  • We'll ship the new item once return is received and inspected

Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be issued if:

  • Product is used or damaged by customer
  • Return request is made after 30 days (for non-defective items)
  • Original packaging/materials are missing
  • Item is significantly different from pictures due to customer misunderstanding of product
  • Return is initiated fraudulently

Disputes & Escalation

If you disagree with a refund decision:

  1. Send detailed photos and explanation to WhatsApp
  2. We'll review and respond within 48 hours
  3. If still unresolved, provide additional clarification
  4. Final decision will be communicated to you

💡 Pro Tip: If you have ANY doubts about a product, ask questions via WhatsApp BEFORE placing your order. We're happy to clarify!

Quality Guarantee

All our products are handcrafted with quality in mind. We stand behind every item sold. If there's a genuine issue with the product, we'll work with you to make it right.

Our goal is your satisfaction. We'd rather solve problems than process refunds!
